The full text is HANDOFF.md §1.1. In short:
- **The premise.** 18 adults live trapped in a gothic dream mansion run by the aquarium-headed butler Yusti. The loop is: daily life → a murder → discovery (3+ witnesses) → investigation → 심판 → verdict and execution → aftermath. The game loops, and only the player and Yusti remember.
- **Emergent, not scripted.** Cases come from simulated motives, relationships and opportunities, yet they must feel like an authored Danganronpa case. Every run needs new cases.
- **Daily life.** Subculture-game bonds; vivid, mature, swearing characters; life goes on without the player; events; murder seeds planted as innocent foreshadowing.
- **Killers.** Proactive and audacious: they seize or create opportunities, prepare, host events, frame others, and even kill during the investigation. Two-layer physical tricks at Kindaichi level. Killing is simulated in the body: prolonged struggles, wounds, broken furniture, dragging.
- **Characters.** AAA physicality: every action has a motion, with natural push, hit and fall reactions; guns and a crossbow; restraint.
- **Investigation.** A shocking discovery film. Stress-free deduction with 10 photo-plates (5 true, 5 misleading) and simple testimony.
- **The 심판** is the heart and must FEEL like people really debating, not a system. Theories clash; the culprit lies, confuses and uses rules as shields; the player turns the flow on contradictions in what people SAY. It is staged in a gothic cosmic-horror court.
- **Quality bar.** Danganronpa as structural reference only; commercial polish; the owner's own music and models used respectfully (their face design must not change).
- **Working style.** Autonomous; test once per finished system; play builds in new folders; honest reporting. Do NOT stop at the listed tasks: keep expanding systems and content by your own judgement (HANDOFF §1.5) and log each expansion in HANDOFF §3.0.
