The owner's current requirement baseline is quoted verbatim in Documentation/BL23/HANDOFF.md §0.5. In short:

1. **Physical, AAA-grade character behaviour.**
   - Pushes, hits and falls look natural: the character reaches for a table when falling and startles.
   - Every body action is animated: knife swings, handing over, leaning on a table, carrying, picking up.
   - Damage depends on the weapon; furniture breaks and moves; residents react in many different ways.
   - Kills take real time: strangling; drowning by the hair with tens of seconds of struggle, pained faces and splashing water.
   - Restraint; firearms and a crossbow.
2. **Daily life with strong character interaction,** as in subculture games.
3. **Proactive killers, Danganronpa style.**
   - An opportunity or event appears, then the killer prepares: trick items, weapons, alibi schemes.
   - Killers host events themselves, frame others from the start, and even kill during the investigation.
   - Endless variety.
4. **A 심판 where residents reason among themselves.**
   - They pile on someone, go on odd tangents, and combine clues.
   - The culprit lies, confuses, rebuts, and uses rules as a shield. Example: when only the first murder can be proven, a second-murder culprit claims no link to the first.
   - These are examples only; the owner wants far more situations and simulation.
5. **Extend further** beyond these.
6. **(2026-09-28 addition)** Daily life and the 심판 must carry the Danganronpa FEEL. **It must feel like a real discussion, not a game system.** This is the quality bar only; never copy names, mechanics or looks, and keep the gothic tone and the name 심판.

**How to apply:**
- Judge every trial or daily-life design by whether it reads as people talking, arguing, interrupting and lying.
- If it reads as menus, card submission or quizzes, it has failed.
- Keep the mature-dialogue freedom and its guardrails.
